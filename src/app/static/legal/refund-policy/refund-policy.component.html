1. The short version

Reading is free and needs no account. Premium is the paid part. If you buy it and change your mind, you get a full refund within 14 days of your first payment. No questions asked.

2. Who you are buying from

Almonium is operated by Almonium LLC, registered in Ukraine under number 46251531. Payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, which acts as the merchant of record. That means your purchase is legally from Paddle, Paddle’s terms of sale also apply, and Paddle is the one that issues your refund.

3. Subscriptions and renewals

Premium renews automatically, monthly or yearly, until you cancel. Cancel from your account page at any time and you keep Premium until the end of the period you have already paid for. There is no trial; the 14 days are what replaces it. After those 14 days, renewal payments are not refunded, except where the law where you live requires it.

A refund ends Premium straight away and your account returns to the free plan. Deleting your account also ends Premium straight away, without a refund, so if you are within the 14 days, ask for the refund first and delete afterwards.

4. Your statutory rights

Nothing on this page takes away a right you have under consumer law. If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal. Elsewhere, local law may give you similar rights. Those rights apply whatever this page says, and where they are more generous than this page, they win.

5. If we change or end the service

If we take something away from a plan you are paying for, we will tell you at least 30 days in advance and you can cancel with a pro-rated refund. If we change the Terms of Use and you do not accept the change, delete your account before it takes effect and we will refund your unused subscription time. If we close your account for breaking the rules, there is no refund. If we shut Almonium down, you get at least 60 days’ notice, a way to export your words, and a refund of any unused subscription time.

6. How to ask

Email support@almonium.com from the address you paid with, or contact Paddle directly at paddle.net. We answer within a few days. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with; how long it takes to appear depends on your bank.
