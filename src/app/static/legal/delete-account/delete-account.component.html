1. How to delete

There are three ways. The first two take a minute; the third is for when you can no longer sign in.

In the app
Settings → Account → Delete account and all data Type your username to confirm. If you haven’t signed in recently, you’ll be asked to sign in again first. The same on iPhone, Android and the web.
On the web
Sign in, then open account settings, where the same button is at the bottom of the page. Sign in and delete
If you can’t sign in
Email support@almonium.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll confirm the request comes from you and delete the account within 30 days.

2. What is deleted

All of it, immediately. When you confirm, we remove:

  • your account and profile, including your display name and avatar
  • your learner profiles and target languages
  • your saved words, decks and review history
  • books you imported, your reading positions and your notes
  • the chat messages you sent and your connections
  • every way you signed in: email and password, Google, Apple

If you have Premium, the subscription is cancelled at the same moment and no further payment is taken.

3. What is kept

A few records outlive the account, for the reasons and periods below. None of them is used to contact you or to rebuild your account.

Payment records
If you ever paid, Paddle keeps the transaction and invoice records. Paddle is the merchant of record and is required by tax law to hold them, independently of your Almonium account. They are covered by Paddle’s own privacy notice.
Server logs
Technical records of requests and errors, which can include your account ID and IP address. They are not kept for a fixed period: each service keeps its most recent 30 MB and older lines are discarded as new ones arrive, which in practice is days to weeks.
Encrypted backups
Database backups made before you deleted your account still contain it until they expire. We keep the last seven daily, four weekly and twelve monthly snapshots, so the oldest copy of a deleted account disappears within twelve months. They are encrypted and used only to recover from a failure.

4. Before you delete

It is permanent. There is no grace period and nothing we can restore. If you sign up again with the same email, you start with an empty account. To keep your words, export them from Settings first.

Your subscription ends immediately, not at the end of the period you have paid for, and the time left is not refunded. If you are within 14 days of your first payment and want the refund described in the Terms of Use, write to us before you delete.

5. Questions

The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why. For anything else, write to support@almonium.com.
