What it is

Almonium turns the books you read into the vocabulary you actually use. You read in your target language with the translation beside it, tap any word to understand it, and Almonium remembers it until you can use it yourself.

No account is needed to start reading.

Who built it

I took my English to C2 on my own, using about a dozen disconnected tools and a lot of patience. Almonium is the tool I wanted. I use it every day.
