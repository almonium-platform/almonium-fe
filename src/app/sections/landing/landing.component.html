See what would sound off to a native speaker

Type a phrase and the fluency trainer marks what a native speaker would notice: a missing article, a word that is close but not the one people use, a structure that is correct but unnatural.

It also offers a more casual and a more formal version and says what changed, and tells you which variety your sentence reads as, with the phrase that gave it away.
